# Areas

**Ongoing responsibilities** — no end date, no outcome. Things you maintain
rather than finish.

- `31 Work/` — your role, team, rituals, recurring processes
- `32 Personal/` — health, finances, home, relationships
- `33 Learning/` — disciplines you're cultivating (Kotlin, Terraform, music)

**Create:** a new note here runs the `area` template (standards, current
focus, related projects, routines).

If it has a deadline, it's a **Project** (`20`), not an Area. See [[Vault Guide]].
