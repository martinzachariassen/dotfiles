# Meta

Vault plumbing.

- `Daily/` — day/week/month notes, organized by year (Periodic Notes)
- `_templates/` — Templater templates (also used by QuickAdd and Periodic Notes)
- `_attachments/` — every pasted image and dropped file lands here (set in `app.json`)
- `Vault Guide` — how the whole vault works → [[Vault Guide]]

Leading underscores keep these pinned at the top of the folder.
