# Daily / Weekly / Monthly

Time-based notes, organized by year. **Periodic Notes** creates them from
their templates on first open.

| Note | Key | Filename |
|---|---|---|
| Daily | `⌘⇧T` | `YYYY-MM-DD.md` |
| Weekly | `⌘⇧W` | `YYYY-Www.md` |
| Monthly | `⌘P → Open monthly note` | `YYYY-MM.md` |

They nest and roll up: each weekly note lists that week's dailies, and each
monthly note lists its weeklies. All live under `YYYY/`. See [[Vault Guide]].
